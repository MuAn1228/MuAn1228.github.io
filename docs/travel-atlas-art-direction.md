# 沐安的山海回响

This personal travel atlas combines aged cartography, quiet signal light, warm miniature landmarks and a visitor's collection of memory seals. The personal material is the author's own travel writing and photographs, not invented travel experiences. Province centres locate symbolic illustrations; they do not claim visits to those precise coordinates. Decorative links are not itineraries.

The visual language draws on exploration, ancient maps, cyberpunk signals and cozy game details. No existing game screenshots, logos, UI exports or character assets are used. This is a custom design, not a promise that a public website cannot be copied or that every motif is globally unique.

The province glyphs, terrain stamps and compass are drawn in project SVG code. The visitor's locally collected seals remain separate from the author's real footprint and article counts.

### Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original background material for a personal travel RPG atlas named Mu An's Echoes of Mountains and Seas. Generate a landscape 3:2 high quality tactile old atlas vellum texture, aged grey jade parchment, charcoal indigo corners, quiet desaturated sage and smoky teal at the center, subtle antique brass flecks, delicate worn fibers, eroded engraving, faint abstract curving topographical etching. A restrained fusion of hand-inked fantasy adventure cartography and almost lost cyan circuit traces. The texture is intended to sit BEHIND an interactive geographic map, so keep all detail low contrast, spacious, nonrepresentational, especially center quiet. Atmospheric, intimate, artisan, weathered but elegant. Warm gold dust and a few teal ghost traces near the edges, no loud neon. Flat top-down material photograph meets hand-painted game concept texture, consistent texture across full canvas, not a 3D scene. No actual map outlines, no continents, no roads, no characters, no buildings, no icons, no emblems, no symbols copied from games, no logos, no compass, no readable text, no lettering, no UI, no borders, no watermark. This should be an original material, not any existing game's map or screenshot.
